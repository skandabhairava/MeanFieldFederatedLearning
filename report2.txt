FEDCAP:

1. Strategic Sybil Attackers can crack the global model. Hence harming the performance

This is multi-dimensional evasion vs. 1D threshold evasion—exponentially harder. Even if they evade, your attract_aggregate uses inverse distance weighting, so outliers at the edge of a cluster have minimal weight.
Experiment to Run: Compare against FedCAP when Tnorm​  is known to the attacker (they optimize to stay just under it). FedCAP should collapse; your accuracy should hold because clustering in 20D is harder to game than a 1D norm check.

2. Natural Client Grouping & Interpretability (NEED to test)
Helpful in running meta-tests on the model params themselves(cluster center), which CAN detect backdoors, which might go unnoticed in FEDCAP, if its within the model ranges.

3. Complexity:
For massive FL (millions of clients), FedCAP's per-client customized aggregation becomes computationally prohibitive. FedAttract's hierarchical approach scales logarithmically

4. In a hierarchy, if a cluster of clients are physically separated. We can create a new Cluster server, closer to the clients. This reduces communication costs.
